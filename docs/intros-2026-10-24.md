# Intros, recaps and credits found in the files (2026-10-24)

`server/src/tv_agent/intros.py`. Why: "skip the intro" found no times in 3 of the voice tests of 2026-10-24
(Breaking Bad S1E6 among them: TheIntroDB and IntroDB had nothing), and in TV Agent Player chapters were never
looked at (only Kodi's `Player.ChapterName` was). Measured on the laptop against the user's addons (AIOStreams on
Premiumize), nothing played on the TV.

## What the files carry

Each copy's Matroska header (one 256 KB range read) for an episode of each show:

| Episode | Copies with named chapters | Others |
|---|---|---|
| Community S2E6 | Netflix 4K (IHATEPEACHES): "Opening Credit" 4:06.3, "Chapter 01" 4:30.9, "End Credits" 21:10.0 | Blu-ray remux and x264: numbered, chapter 2 at 4:32.4 (the intro's end); four web copies: none |
| Breaking Bad S2E3 | Netflix 4K (two groups): "Intro"/"Opening Credits" 3:35.5–3:53.2, "Credits"/"End Credits" 46:14.4; Amazon: scene names, the intro being the one named "Breaking Bad" (3:36–3:54), "End Credits" 46:15 | Blu-ray remuxes: numbered, chapter 2 at 3:54.0, chapter 6 at 46:15.2; seven web copies: none |
| Pluribus S1E3 | Apple TV+ copies from REPACK, NTb and Neos: "Intro" 6:23.8–6:48.8, "End Credits" 42:01.9. FLUX (three releases): "Studio Logo", "Recap" 0:08.0–0:49.4, "Skip", "Intro" 7:19.2–7:44.2, "Credits" 41:29.0 | Kitsune, playWEB, G66: none; an Italian copy: a chapter every 5 min |
| The Bear S2E3, S2E4 | Disney+ (FLUX) and Hulu (HONE, BlackTV, NTb…): "Credits" only (25:17.4) | Disney+ 1080p (NTb), ETHEL: none |

So the services' own markers are in most 4K web releases, and the intro and credits come at no cost. Two findings:

- **One group's chapters can be wrong.** FLUX's Pluribus S1E3 intro is 55.4 s late and its credits 32.9 s early,
  in all three of its releases; the other groups agree with each other and with the sound (the season's theme
  is at 6:25.5). So a chapter counts as **sure** only when two release groups agree (within 2 s at both ends), or
  when the season's intro sound is there.
- A service that marks the credits and no intro means there's none: The Bear has only a short title card, and
  Community's bottle episode S2E8 has no opening (Netflix marks none, and the sound finds none).

## How a file's markers are found (cheapest first)

1. **Its own chapters** at once (one header read when it starts): the Skip intro button and a skip when asked inside
   them. Names: intro, opening (credits/titles/theme/sequence), OP, main titles, title sequence, theme (song); recap,
   previously (on…); (end/ending/closing/final) credits, ending, outro, ED, end titles; Amazon's chapter named after
   the show in the first quarter.
2. **Other copies' headers**, ~20 s after the start (the TV has buffered): at most 6, one per release group first,
   service releases (NF, AMZN, ATVP, DSNP, HULU…) first, until two groups agree. A copy as long as this one (within
   150 ms) is the same cut, its times are this file's. Another cut's markers are moved by sound: 12–20 s of its sound
   at the marker found in this file within the length difference ±4 s.
3. **The season's intro sound.** Learned once per season, kept in `~/.local/share/tv-agent/intros/<imdb>.json`:
   - from intro chapters that sound the same (this episode's and two neighbours', ~30 s of sound each), else
   - from the starts of this episode and two neighbours (their cheapest copies, by the bitrate their index shows:
     an addon's size can be a season pack's), read 5 minutes more at a time up to a third of the episode (at most
     15 min): the longest stretch (8–180 s, not a studio logo at 0:00) all of them share, or two of three if it's
     at least 15 s and clean (a recap repeats the last episode's scenes).
   With it, an intro chapter is checked by a 15 s read where it says, and an episode without one is read from its
   start, 150 s at a time, until the sound is there. A season where nothing is shared isn't tried again for a week.
4. **Credits by sound**: only the studios' logos at the very end are shared (the end music changes from one episode
   to the next in all four shows), so a marker from sound is 1–2 s (Community) to 30–55 s (Breaking Bad, Pluribus,
   The Bear) into the credits.
5. TheIntroDB and IntroDB (skips.py) for what's still missing.

Sound is compared by a Haitsma–Kalker fingerprint, numpy only (no new dependency): 5512 Hz mono from ffmpeg, frames of
0.37 s every 0.05 s, 33 bands from 300 to 2000 Hz, 32 bits per step (the signs of the band-to-band energy differences
from one step to the next). The same sound in two encodes differs in 2–6 bits, unrelated sound in ~16, silence matches
nothing. Shifts are found by counting equal 16-bit halves per shift, then each candidate shift's bit errors are
smoothed over a second: under 10 bits is the same sound. 10 minutes of sound: ~0.3 s of numpy (in a thread), the
comparison ~10 ms.

The sound of a stretch: the clusters between the index points around it, one range read at most 80 Mbit/s, waiting
while TV Agent Player has under 20 s buffered, piped into ffmpeg (nice 15, one thread) after the file's header, the
one audio track decoded (English, else the default). The link is never on ffmpeg's command line or in a log.

**Reading only the audio isn't possible** over range reads: a Matroska cluster interleaves every track's blocks in
time order (in Breaking Bad's 21 Mbit/s Netflix 4K: 24 video frames a second of ~110 KB each, and between them the
sound's blocks, DD+ at 640 kbit/s in frames of 32 ms, ~2.5 KB), and a block's place is only known from the one
before it. Skipping the video would take a range request per block, some 50 for each second of film. So the cheapest copy's whole clusters are read instead: 1.9–5 Mbit/s for these
shows, against 10–60 Mbit/s for the copies played.

## Accuracy

The sound alone (step 4 above, without chapters), against the services' chapters, 16 episodes:

| Show | Intros found | Start | End |
|---|---|---|---|
| Breaking Bad S2E2–5 | 4 of 4 | −0.2 to +0.6 s | 1.5–2.3 s early |
| Community S2E5–8 | 3 of 3, and none in S2E8 (it has none) | 0.7–0.8 s early | 1.6–1.8 s late |
| Pluribus S1E2–5 | 4 of 4 (S1E4's starts at 10:13: found with the 15-minute window) | 1.2–2.3 s late | 3.0–4.9 s early |
| The Bear S2E2–5 | none, as the services (a title card only) | | |

The shared sound is the theme itself; the services' marks sit a little around it (Community, Netflix inside it by
~1 s; Pluribus, Apple outside it by 2–5 s). Skipping to the sound's end lands at most ~1.8 s past Netflix's mark,
still before the first line (Community S2E6: theme ends 4:32.6, next line 4:34.9).

Moving another cut's markers by sound (Breaking Bad S2E3, Netflix's chapters onto the Blu-ray remux): the intro
moved +0.77 s, ending 0.26 s from the remux's own chapter 2; the credits +5.28 s (a 24 s stretch matching at 2 bits).

## Cost

`Finder.find` end to end on real files (`intros.py`, laptop, reads capped at 80 Mbit/s):

| Case | Read | Time | ffmpeg CPU |
|---|---|---|---|
| The Bear S2E3, Disney+ 4K: credits, two groups agree; no intro | 1.6 MB | 6.6 s | 0 |
| Pluribus S1E3, FLUX 1080p: its wrong intro and credits outvoted | 4.2 MB | 11.6 s | 0 |
| Community S2E6, Netflix 4K: one group, its intro sounds as S2E7's intro chapter | 82 MB | 19 s | 0.3 s |
| Breaking Bad S2E3, Blu-ray remux: Netflix's chapters moved by sound, confirmed by S2E4's | 230 MB | 39 s | 1.8 s |
| The same as if no copy had named chapters: the season learned from three starts | 494 MB | 84 s | 6.5 s |
| Then Breaking Bad S2E5's remux, the season known | 217 MB | 44 s | 2.4 s |
| Pluribus S1E4 4K, no named chapters, a 10-minute cold open (worst case) | 1.3 GB | 159 s | 8.8 s |

The header-only cases are mostly the addons' stream lookups and link resolves. A 4K episode played is 2–9 GB, so the
worst case adds ~15% once per season; the usual cases nothing measurable. All of it runs after the start (the file's
own chapters at once, the rest 20 s later) and for the next episode while this one plays.

Resolving many links in a few minutes made AIOStreams answer with its `/status/video/error.mp4` (not caught before:
`streams.PROBLEM_VIDEO` now does), so the reads per episode are capped and what's found is kept per file.

## Used for

- "Skip the intro / recap": inside it, a jump to its end; before a sure one, "The intro starts at 6:23: skipping it
  then"; after, "Already past the intro". One group's chapters alone only skip when you're in them.
- auto_skip: sure intros and recaps. TV Agent Player skips them itself when it says it shows markers (hello
  `features: ["markers"]`), else the agent does as before.
- TV Agent Player: the markers with the `load`/`preload` and later (`markers`), its Skip intro button and the Next
  episode card at the credits, counting down 10 s with binge on and the credits sure; the next episode is preloaded
  by then (30 s before the credits, or 150 s before the end). Protocol: the player's PROTOCOL.md (requested
  2026-10-24).
